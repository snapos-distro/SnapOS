SnapGuard comes alive.

Snappy now has a cup next to him in the SnapGuard window:

- **A threat is found:** the cup comes up and the virus falls into it.
- **While it is in quarantine:** the virus jumps around in the cup, angry.
- **Delete:** Snappy tips the cup and eats the virus.
- **Trust:** the virus grows wings and flies away.

This update comes only through `snapos update` (from snapos-updates); the installer images stay at 2.5.1.
